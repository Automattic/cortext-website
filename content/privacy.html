Cortext.digital is a small product site and blog. There is no account area and nothing to fill in.

What this site counts

This site counts page views through Jetpack Stats, which records the page requested, a truncated IP address, and basic browser information. We use those counts to see which pages people read. They are not tied to an identity and we do not combine them with anything else.

There are no contact forms, no newsletter sign-ups and no public comments. We do not build advertising profiles and we do not sell visitor data.

WordPress.com and Jetpack

This site runs on WordPress.com and uses services from WordPress.com and Jetpack. Those services process technical information needed to deliver, measure and protect the site, such as an IP address, browser and device details, and request logs.

Automattic describes that processing, the reasons for it, and the choices available to visitors in its Privacy Notice.

The software is not this website

Everything above is about cortext.digital. Cortext itself makes no analytics calls and sends no telemetry. The only request it makes outside your own site is the optional Notion import, and only when you start one.

Links to other services

Links to GitHub, WordPress.org, WordPress Playground, the Cortext roadmap and other websites take you to sites we do not run. Their own terms apply once you get there.
