Knowledge has a habit of spreading. Notes end up in documents, projects in tables, decisions in chat, and the useful links between them depend on somebody remembering where to look.

Cortext is our attempt to put writing and structure in the same workspace while keeping the underlying data open. Version 0.2 makes those parts feel much more connected.

Why “Cortext”?

The name combines cortex and text. Text is the durable part: notes, records, and decisions that you can read and revise. Cortex is everything around them, the links that turn a pile of notes into something worth keeping.

Underneath the interface it is all ordinary WordPress content, sitting in a database you control. You can read it, export it, and host it where you like, with or without Cortext.

What changed in 0.2

This release adds the connections and calculated fields that were missing from the first beta, then does a fair amount of work behind the scenes to keep larger workspaces responsive.

Mentions and backlinks

You can mention another document while writing and open it from the editor. Cortext records that reference as a backlink on the mentioned document, so the trail works in both directions.

Formula fields

Collections can now calculate values from their other fields. A formula can turn raw dates, numbers, and text into a result that stays with the record and appears in its views.

Less waiting in larger workspaces

The sidebar tree now loads pages only when they are needed and paginates long lists. Collection rows can use a smaller, ID-only response, and table footer totals are calculated on the server. These changes reduce the amount of data the browser has to fetch and process.

A sturdier desktop app

The Mac app updates itself now. The rest of this cycle went into parts you should never have to notice: keeping profiles apart, tightening what the packaged app can reach, and getting the build running on Windows as well as macOS. There is no Windows app to download yet, but that is where it is heading.

The full list, including links to every merged change, is in the Cortext 0.2.0 release notes.

What beta means here

The data model and the interface are still moving. Put Cortext somewhere low-stakes, keep a backup, and read the release notes before upgrading. Beta here means things will change under you, not that they will vanish.
